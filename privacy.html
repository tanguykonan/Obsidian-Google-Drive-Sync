Privacy Policy

Google Drive Sync Plus (Obsidian Community Plugin) • Last updated: March 2026

Introduction and Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Google Drive Sync Plus ("the Plugin", "we", "our"), an open-source community plugin for Obsidian developed by Tanguy Konan, handles your personal data, vault files, and Google account credentials.

Our core philosophy is built upon user sovereignty, local-first computing, and privacy by design: we do not operate an intermediary backend server, we do not collect or monitor your notes, and we do not track or sell any user information.

1. Direct Client-to-Cloud Architecture (No Middleman Server)

Google Drive Sync Plus operates entirely within your local Obsidian client:

  • Direct Communication: All synchronization and API calls occur directly between your device and Google's official servers (https://www.googleapis.com) via secure HTTPS requests.
  • No Middleman Server: We do not operate or maintain any intermediate proxy, database, or analytics server. Your notes, metadata, and authentication tokens never pass through or touch any third-party server managed by the developer.
  • Zero Telemetry: The Plugin does not contain any analytics libraries, telemetry trackers, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising code.

2. Google Account Data and Requested Permissions

To synchronize your Obsidian vault with Google Drive, the Plugin requests access using Google OAuth 2.0 PKCE:

  • Scope Requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (View and manage Google Drive files and folders that you have opened or created with this app).
  • Limited Access: The Plugin has access only to the files, folders, and synchronization metadata that it creates itself within your Google Drive. It cannot browse, read, or modify unrelated documents, spreadsheets, photos, or files residing on your Google Drive.
  • Credential Storage: Your Google OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) are stored strictly on your local device inside your Obsidian vault configuration folder (.obsidian/plugins/google-drive-sync-plus/data.json). They are never shared, exported, or transmitted to anyone other than Google's OAuth endpoint.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance
Google Drive Sync Plus's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Google User Data: Limited Use and Protection

In accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • We do not use Google user data to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or external entities.
  • Human access to your data is non-existent: no human (including the developer) can read or inspect your notes or Google Drive files through the Plugin.

4. Zero-Knowledge End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

The Plugin includes an optional client-side End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) feature:

  • When enabled, all note contents and file data are encrypted locally on your device using AES-256-GCM and a key derived via PBKDF2-SHA256 (with unique salts and initialization vectors) prior to being uploaded to Google Drive.
  • Only encrypted ciphertext is stored on Google Drive. Neither Google nor the developer holds or has access to your master password or encryption keys.
  • Decryption takes place exclusively on your local device with the password you provide.

5. User Control, Token Revocation, and Data Deletion

You have full and continuous control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnect Account: You can disconnect your Google account at any time in the Plugin settings. This immediately erases your stored authentication tokens from your local vault.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can revoke the Plugin's permission to your Google Drive at any time through your Google Account Security Settings.
  • Delete Sync Data: You can permanently delete any files synchronized to your Google Drive directly from the Google Drive web or mobile interface, or by deleting them within Obsidian.
  • Plugin Uninstall: Removing the Plugin from your Obsidian vault deletes all local configuration, cached tokens, and sync history.

6. Children's Privacy

The Plugin is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any updates or modifications to this Privacy Policy will be posted to this page with an updated modification date. Since the Plugin does not maintain user accounts or email lists, we encourage you to review this page periodically.
